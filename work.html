WRK/01 — ORIGINAL BUILD

OT Vulnerability Lab — see every device, prove every weakness.

An interactive OT/ICS vulnerability-assessment console covering the full Phase-3 workflow: CPE-to-CVE association against live NVD data, real-time CVSS interpretation, and OpenVAS/Nuclei scanner adapters gated behind explicit authorization. Risk is prioritized on severity, EPSS, CISA KEV status, exposure, and device role — so a safety PLC always outranks a camera carrying the same CVE. Every finding maps to NIST CSF 2.0, SP 800-53, IEC 62443, and MITRE ATT&CK for ICS, and evidence is tracked as candidate-vs-confirmed rather than taken as ground truth.
