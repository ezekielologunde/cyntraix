04 — Services Provided

Eight practices, one standard.

Every service is delivered to the same internal spec — documented, auditable, and repeatable. No two clients are the same; the rigor is. Select a practice to expand it.

  • S.01 Information Security Threat modeling · Hardening↗

    End-to-end protection for systems, networks, and data — threat modeling, control design, and continuous validation against real adversary behavior.

  • S.02 Information Management Classification · Lifecycle↗

    Governance over how information is classified, stored, retained, and retired — so every record has a known owner and a known lifecycle.

  • S.03 Software Testing QA · Security testing↗

    Functional and adversarial testing side by side — so quality and security regressions are caught in the same pass, before release.

  • S.04 Project Management Delivery · Risk tracking↗

    Structured delivery for security and IT initiatives — scoped milestones, tracked risk, and a single accountable timeline.

  • S.05 Technical Support Response · Remediation↗

    Responsive, senior-level technical support for the systems that matter most — with clear escalation paths and no first-line triage maze.

  • S.06 Customer Support Client success↗

    Direct access to the people who understand your environment — not a ticket queue. Every engagement includes a named point of contact.

  • S.07 Educational Consulting Training · Enablement↗

    Security literacy for your team — tailored training, tabletop exercises, and enablement so good practice outlasts the engagement.

  • S.08 IT Law Compliance · Policy↗

    Guidance at the intersection of technology and regulation — data-protection compliance, policy drafting, and audit readiness.
